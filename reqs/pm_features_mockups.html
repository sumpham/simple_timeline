Planning features for simple timeline

Mockups for deadlines, named baselines, earned value, levelling people, links between projects, and a review page for every assistant suggestion, drawn in the app’s own type and colours. Red still means a double-booking and nothing new; every new state is shown with ink weight, a word, or a line style. The decisions behind each screen are in reqs/pm_features.md.

Deadlines

A date a task must finish by. It never moves the task; it eats its float, so a committed date can make a task critical.

  • A small ink chevron marks the deadline on the row.
  • A late finish gets a bracket under the overrun and a “+2d”. No hatch: hatching means a double-booking.
  • The table reads the slack in words, with the existing late tag.
  • In the editor, Use current finish commits to what the plan says today.

Named baselines

Keep up to ten saved plans and pick the one to compare against. The picker sits in the facts row, where the old baseline date was.

  • The slip chart plots each baseline’s finish date. A line that keeps rising is a plan that keeps slipping.
  • Click a row to compare with it. Ghost bars, variance columns and earned value all follow.
  • Save current plan as… is one field and one button.

Earned value

A Budget tab that answers two questions at a status date: are we behind, and are we over? It answers in sentences first, and the indices sit underneath.

  • Lines are told apart by stroke (dashed, solid, dotted) and named at their ends. There’s no legend to decode.
  • An index below 0.9 turns bold. The colour doesn’t change.
  • Spent is marked “est.” until someone types an actual cost.

Levelling people

The app already spots a person on two tasks at once. Now the assistant suggests the fix, and you choose whether to apply it.

  • The People strip under the chart shows each person’s load per day. Two tasks at once is a tall ink block marked “2×”.
  • A suggestion shows on the chart as a dashed ghost bar before you apply it.
  • Within float, the finish holds. Moves beyond float show their cost and appear only in the aggressive profile.

Links between projects

A task in one project drives a task in another. You type it where you already type links, in After: Payments:12.

  • Autocomplete suggests projects after the first letter and tasks after the colon.
  • A link to another project is a chip with an ink outline. Clicking it opens that plan at that task.
  • On the chart, a short stub arrow enters the bar, so it never draws a line to a row that isn’t there.
  • Before you save, the impact banner lists each downstream project, including any double-booking the move would cause.

Review a suggestion

Every suggestion card gets Review. It opens the plan as it would be, so you can check it from every side before pressing Apply.

  • Sentences come first: what gets better, and anything that gets worse, listed first and in bold.
  • The Timeline view draws the to-be plan solid and today’s plan as a dashed outline. Changed rows carry an ink rule and the size of the change.
  • Untick a move to see the plan without it. If that opens a double-booking, the page says so.
  • Environments, People, Budget and Other projects show the same change from their side.
  • Nothing is saved until Apply, and Apply uses the same save as the card does.
